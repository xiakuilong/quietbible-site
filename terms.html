Quiet Bible Reader is designed for personal reading. It has no sharing, community, donation or outreach tools, and it must not be used to redistribute scripture as a separate product.

1. What this app is

Quiet Bible Reader is a local-first Bible reading app for personal use. Bible text is provided in the World English Bible (WEB), the Berean Standard Bible (BSB) and Alkitab Yang Terbuka (AYT). The app does not provide pastoral advice, doctrine, counselling or emergency services.

4. Quiet Pro

Quiet Pro is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks additional reading tools. It is not a subscription. It does not lock the Bible text or basic reading features. In the App Store version, purchases are processed by Apple and are subject to Apple’s terms; restore is handled through the App Store. In this prototype, the purchase is simulated and no payment is taken.
