Common questions

How do I read offline?

Open the library from the translation chip in the reader, then choose a book to download, or tap “Download all” for a whole Bible. Once downloaded, the text and search index work without a connection.

Which translations are included?

World English Bible (WEB) and Berean Standard Bible (BSB) in English, and Alkitab Yang Terbuka (AYT) in Indonesian. WEB and BSB are public domain. AYT is used for personal, non-commercial reading under CC BY-NC-SA.

Is my reading data uploaded?

No. There is no account, no analytics and no cloud sync. Bookmarks, highlights, notes and your reading position stay on your device. See the privacy policy.

How do I restore Quiet Pro?

Open Settings → Quiet Pro and tap “Restore Purchases” on the purchase screen. Quiet Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase tied to your Apple Account. It is not a subscription.

How do I back up or move my marks?

Quiet Pro can export a local JSON backup and restore it on another device. There is no automatic cloud backup; the file is yours to store.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to remove its local library, bookmarks, highlights, notes and settings. Because nothing is stored on a server, there is no account to delete.

Why does AYT show a licence note?

AYT is © 2011–2024 YLSA-AYT and is distributed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for personal, non-commercial reading. The app does not sell or sublicense the AYT text. Commercial redistribution requires written permission from YLSA.
